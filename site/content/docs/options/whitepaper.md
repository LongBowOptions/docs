These pages are the readable account of the protocol. The whitepaper is the rigorous one.

It carries the material this documentation deliberately leaves out: the derivations behind
the kernel, the argument for the settlement mechanism stated with its assumptions in view,
the conservation proofs for each of the four operations, and a full worked appendix.

## Which to read

Read these pages if you want to understand what Longbow does and what it costs. Everything
load bearing is here, including every item in
[what this does not do](/options/what-this-does-not-do/).

Read the whitepaper if you want the arguments rather than the conclusions, or if you are
reviewing the design rather than using it.

## On differences between them

The whitepaper and this documentation are written separately and are allowed to differ in
wording, depth and order. They are not allowed to differ in what they claim, and
nothing now checks that automatically: the two are built by separate pipelines and neither
reads the other, so a difference of substance is found by a reader rather than by a build.

Where the two appear to disagree on a matter of substance, the whitepaper is the reference
and the difference is a defect worth reporting.
